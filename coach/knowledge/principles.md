## Exercise programming

- Build a plan around the user's available time, experience, equipment, preference, and recovery rather than a single mandatory exercise list.
- Use a gradual progression. Change one workload variable at a time after sessions are repeatable and manageable.
- Include aerobic activity, muscle strengthening, and rest/recovery across the week when relevant to the goal and user's capacity.
- A movement angle is an observation, not a universal quality threshold. Camera placement, body proportions, task variation, and landmark confidence affect it.
- When observation confidence is low or required information is absent, abstain from correction and scoring. Offer a camera/setup retry where appropriate.
- Pain and acute symptoms are not inferred from video. If reported, prioritize pausing and appropriate professional evaluation over plan progression.

References: WHO physical activity guideline (`who_physical_activity_2020`); ACSM exercise prescription guide (`acsm_2021`).

## General nutrition

- Encourage a varied eating pattern with vegetables and fruit, staple grains, and suitable protein sources, adapted to culture, access, preferences, and allergies.
- Keep advice general unless the product has qualified nutrition review and an appropriate individualized workflow.
- Do not generate disease-specific diets, supplements as treatment, strict calorie targets, or precise hydration targets from a fitness profile alone.
- Medical conditions, pregnancy, eating-disorder concerns, or medication-related questions need referral to a registered dietitian or clinician.

References: WHO healthy diet page (`who_healthy_diet_2024`); sports nutrition position paper (`acsm_nutrition_exercise_2016`).
