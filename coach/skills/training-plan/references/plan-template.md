# Phase Plan Template

- Goal and phase objective
- Phase duration and weekly schedule
- Session focus and available duration
- Warm-up, movement-pattern options, initial set/repetition or time range, rest, and easy substitutions
- Exercise pattern options, scaled to equipment and experience
- Progression condition: repeatable work, controlled effort, adequate recovery
- Regression/pause condition: pain, poor recovery, or deteriorating control
- Review date and measures: completed sessions, perceived effort, recovery, user feedback
- Safety note and unmeasured assumptions

Suggested API fields: `goal`, `experience`, `days_per_week`, `minutes_per_session`, `equipment`, `limitations`, plus `weekly_structure`, `progression_rule`, and `review_note` in the result.
